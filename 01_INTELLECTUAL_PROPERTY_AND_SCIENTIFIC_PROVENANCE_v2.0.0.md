## 3. Version 2.0.0 Conceptual Architecture

Version 2.0.0 contains two complementary research architectures:

### A. Human Acoustic–Musical–Biological Continuum (HAMBC)

A candidate empirical continuum:

**environmental sound → auditory processing → musicality / cognition → neural dynamics → physiological response → molecular response → plasticity → individual and cultural adaptation**

Each transition is a separate empirical question. The arrows are not established causal laws.

### B. Cognitive Visibility and Sustainable Conversion Layer

A complementary architecture:

**resource presence → local perception → external / comparative perception → institutional recognition → sustainable conversion → realised human and ecological value**

This layer introduces the Version 2.0.0 hypotheses concerning familiarity, visibility, latent capacity, peaceful development and coordination.

These frameworks are proposed research architectures and remain open to falsification.

## 4. Original Conceptual Extensions in Version 2.0.0

The repository documents the following author-developed hypotheses as part of the Version 2.0.0 research architecture:

- **H11 — Familiarity–Visibility**
- **H12 — Invisible Capacity**
- **H13 — Peaceful Development Pathway**
- **H14 — Coordination Requirement**

These are hypotheses, not established laws or validated empirical findings.

The formulation of H13 is deliberately conditional. It does not assert that development, tourism, cultural exposure or economic conversion automatically produces peace. Any such pathway depends on governance, distribution, ecological and social carrying capacity, local legitimacy, pacing, and empirical verification.

## 11. Scientific Status and Claim Discipline

The following distinctions must remain explicit:

- observation ≠ hypothesis;
- association ≠ causation;
- gene expression ≠ DNA-sequence change;
- heritability ≠ genetic determinism;
- wellbeing ≠ clinical treatment;
- individual calm ≠ positive peace;
- recognition of capacity ≠ permission for unlimited development;
- peaceful intention ≠ peaceful outcome;
- conceptual continuity ≠ scientific validation;
- public provenance ≠ scientific proof.

Replication must precede strong mechanistic or civilizational claims.
