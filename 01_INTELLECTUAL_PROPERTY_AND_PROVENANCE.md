## 3. Project-specific terminology

- **CMC Gate:** Civilizational Meta-Cognition Gate.
- **DHAM-CO:** Dynamic Artistic Human Meta-Cognition.
- **HMC-GATE:** Human Meta-Cognition Gate.
- **Passivity:** in the Version 1.2.0 lineage, a proposed systemic condition in which recognition, warning, or stated commitment is not converted into sufficiently timely and accountable action.
- **Civilian Compression:** a proposed construct describing the progressive narrowing of civilian safety, options, access, and protective space under interacting pressures.
- **Communication-Bridge Failure:** a proposed construct concerning breakdowns or distortions in channels connecting affected people, knowledge holders, institutions, and decision centres.
- **Fragmentation of Decision Topology:** a proposed description of dispersed, disconnected, overlapping, or poorly coordinated decision relationships.

These terms are author-proposed working constructs unless independently established by the relevant literature. They must not be presented as standardized measures.

## 6. Scope and limitations

The project is conceptual, interdisciplinary, systems-oriented, hypothesis-generating, and pre-empirical. The warning that passivity could lead humanity to extinction is an authorial rhetorical warning, not a quantified forecast. Likewise, the proposition that truth-telling should strengthen hope and agency is an ethical and research proposition to be investigated, not a universal psychological guarantee.
