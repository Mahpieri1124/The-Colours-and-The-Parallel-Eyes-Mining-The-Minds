## What this repository is

This repository introduces a peace-and-security research module that extends **BAcc-NOVELTY — Balanced Acceleration of Novelty in Harmony with Hightech Revolution** into the study of hybrid conflict, civilian consequences, and institutional adaptation.

The central concern is a growing mismatch between the speed, scale, and complexity of hybrid competition and the ability of peace-oriented institutions to recognize, interpret, contest, and respond to its consequences for civilians. The module asks how decision authority and influence are distributed across centralized, semi-centralized, and decentralized arrangements, and how those arrangements affect escalation, accountability, public trust, essential services, and the conditions associated with Positive Peace.

## Research architecture

**Hybrid-system observation (public-interest, non-operational) → multi-perspective interpretation → civilian-impact assessment → HMC-GATE challenge → BAcc-NOVELTY decision review → institutional response options → monitoring → learning and adaptation.**

The architecture is multi-level and iterative. It does not assume that decision-making is always located in one command centre. Authority, data, influence, and responsibility may be distributed differently and may change over time. The research should map these patterns at an institutional and societal level, not identify exploitable operational vulnerabilities.

## Key distinctions

- **Hybrid threat / hybrid warfare:** policy and analytical terminology whose definitions vary. It is not, by itself, a legal classification of armed conflict.
- **Centralized / semi-centralized / decentralized:** analytical descriptions of decision-authority topology. Real systems may be mixed, nested, and time-varying.
- **Intelligence gathering:** examined as an information-governance and civilian-risk issue, not as a practical intelligence tradecraft manual.
- **Positive Peace:** conditions, institutions, and social structures that support peaceful societies; not merely the absence of direct violence.
- **BAcc-NOVELTY:** a proposed decision principle balancing useful innovation with evidence, uncertainty, reversibility, safeguards, human agency, institutional readiness, and learning capacity.

## Research status and limitations

This is a conceptual, hypothesis-generating, pre-empirical framework. No causal effect, predictive accuracy, operational capability, or institutional effectiveness is claimed. The four “Dynamical Hybrid…” labels are author-supplied provisional constructs. Their definitions in this repository are working interpretations to support discussion and must be checked with the author before formal operationalization.
